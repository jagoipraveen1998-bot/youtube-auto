# The New House
I moved in on a Monday. The rent was suspiciously cheap.
The estate agent said the last tenant just left. With all his furniture. And his coffee mug. Still warm.
MUM: Did you lock the back door?
ME: Mum, I always lock the back door.
That night, I lay in bed and listened to the house settle.

# The First Night
At three in the morning, someone knocked. Not on the front door.
On the wardrobe. From the inside.
I did what any brave adult would do. I hid under the blanket and pretended to be a pillow.
THE DOLL: You're in my bed.
I checked the security camera on my laptop.

# The Footage
The footage showed me, asleep in bed, snoring like a broken fridge.
And a little porcelain doll standing beside me, holding my phone.
Then my phone buzzed. A message from an unknown number.
It said: you snore.
The doll turned to the camera and smiled.
I moved out on Tuesday. The rent was not cheap enough.
