# The Fourth Face

*3-minute horror-comedy short · Shooting and editing script*

**Cast:** Joe, Maya, Ben, Malik (Joe’s Black friend), and Dad (voice only).

## 0:00–0:30 — The photo

**INT. CAR — NIGHT**

**0:00–0:12 · Dashboard shot.** Maya drives down a quiet road. Ben reaches for the radio. Joe sits in the back. Frame Joe tightly enough to keep the seat beside him out of view.

**BEN:** You know we’ve heard this song three times.

**MAYA:** And you’ve skipped it before the good part three times.

**0:12–0:23 · Selfie angle.** Joe raises his phone. Ben leans into frame. Joe takes the photo just as a streetlight sweeps across the car. Three faces are clear. A fourth face is blurred against the far window.

**0:23–0:30 · Phone insert.** Joe sends the photo to Dad with the message *On our way home.*

*Edit and sound:* Show the photo for about two seconds. Cut forward a few minutes on the send sound. The radio is quiet now. Joe’s phone vibrates. **DAD CALLING.**

## 0:30–1:18 — Dad’s warning

**0:30–0:57 · Close on Joe.** He answers with a smile.

**JOE:** Hey, Dad. We’re about—

**DAD** *(low, breathless)*: Joe. Listen to me. I need you to get out of that car right now.

**JOE:** What happened? Why do you sound scared?

**DAD:** I’ll explain when you’re out. Tell Maya to pull over somewhere safe.

**JOE:** Dad, you’re freaking me out.

*Hold on Joe for two seconds. Let Dad’s breathing come through the phone.*

**DAD** *(almost whispering)*: Keep looking straight ahead, okay?

**0:57–1:18 · Rearview mirror, then windshield.** Joe meets Maya’s eyes in the mirror. A closed gas station comes into view.

**JOE:** Maya. The gas station up there. Pull in.

**EXT. CLOSED GAS STATION — NIGHT**

*Cut to a wide shot as Maya parks under the one working light. Cut the engine sound. Joe, Maya, and Ben get out. Three doors slam. The far rear door stays shut.*

**MAYA:** What did he say?

Before Joe can answer, his phone buzzes.

## 1:18–2:08 — The fourth face

**1:18–1:39 · Over Joe’s shoulder.** Dad has sent back the same selfie with a shaky red circle around the face behind Joe. Joe puts the call on speaker and pinches to zoom. Two eyes. The suggestion of a mouth. A sliver of white earbud cord at the edge of the circle.

**DAD:** Right behind you. Do you see it?

Joe zooms in farther.

**DAD:** Son, there’s a ghost in that car.

**1:39–1:56 · Slow move toward the rear window.** Joe switches on his phone flashlight and takes a step closer.

**BEN:** Joe. What are you doing?

The beam slides across the glass. Something shifts inside.

**KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK.**

*Sound and edit:* Hold on the glass for all three knocks. Cut to Joe flinching after the third. Leave the hum of the gas station light underneath.

**1:56–2:08 · Low angle on the rear door.** The handle clicks. The door creaks open. A sneaker plants itself on the pavement. Tilt up to Malik in a black hoodie, blinking in the light with an earbud in and a crease on his cheek.

**MALIK** *(sleepy)*: Why’d you guys get out? Are we getting snacks?

## 2:08–2:36 — The second photo

**2:08–2:21 · Medium shot.** Joe spots the white earbud cord and looks back at the photo.

**JOE:** Dad. That’s Malik. We picked him up after we left.

**DAD:** Then why did he look like that?

Malik leans over Joe’s shoulder to see the screen.

**MALIK:** Because your son takes pictures like he’s being chased.

*Let Ben’s laugh break the tension. Maya switches on the dome light.*

**MAYA:** Take another one before he calls a priest.

**2:21–2:36 · Selfie angle, then phone insert.** All four squeeze into frame. Malik gives a big, deliberate smile. Joe takes the picture and sends it. Dad’s reply pops up.

*DAD: Hi Malik. Sorry.*

Malik leans toward the phone and whispers into the still-open call.

**MALIK:** I forgive you.

**DAD:** Don’t do that.

Joe laughs and hangs up.

## 2:36–3:00 — Final scene · Private production pages

**2:36–2:43 · Phone close-up.** Joe’s phone rings again. The screen reads **MALIK — INCOMING CALL**. Hold on the name for two beats. Cut to Joe. His smile drops. He looks at the person beside him, then back at the phone.

**2:43–2:51 · Tight on Joe.** He answers with a shaky thumb.

**JOE** *(barely audible)*: Hello?

**MALIK** *(through the phone, tired and casual)*: Sorry, Joe. I got sick, so I won’t come.

*Sound:* Use a light phone filter. Let the line land, then hold for a beat with the hum of the overhead light.

**2:51–3:00 · Over Joe’s shoulder.** Joe slowly turns. The figure beside him tilts its head. The same smile from the selfie stretches too wide. Joe freezes as the skinwalker laughs in his face.

**SKINWALKER:** Haa… haa… HAA… HAAAA!

*Edit and sound:* Record the laugh separately and place it beside Joe in the mix. Layer in a lower take as it grows. Cut to black mid-laugh. Let the laugh carry over black for one beat, then cut the audio at **3:00**.

*Sharing note:* The public excerpt ends when Joe hangs up on Dad. Keep the final scene in the private production copy.
